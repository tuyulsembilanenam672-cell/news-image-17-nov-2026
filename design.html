TUESDAY, 17 NOVEMBER 2026
Seven stories that defined the day — from the Kerch Strait to Shenzhen
LEAD STORY · EUROPE
UKRAINE BRINGS DOWN THE CRIMEA BRIDGE ARCH
A second strike in two days drops the Kerch Bridge’s road arch into the strait. The rail span stands damaged and traffic is halted. Kyiv calls it payback for Russian strikes on its infrastructure; Moscow’s missiles hit Kyiv’s river bridges.
MIDDLE EAST
TANKERS STRUCK NEAR STRAIT OF HORMUZ
Oil tankers are hit and catch fire by the strait as tensions rise. An Iranian drone attack on a U.S. radar base in Bahrain is intercepted, with no damage.
DIPLOMACY · ASIA
TRUMP, XI AND PUTIN TO MEET IN SHENZHEN
China confirms the first three-way meeting of the three leaders, on Thursday at the APEC summit. Trump is already on his way to Asia.
EUROPE · MIGRATION
FIVE NATIONS PLAN RWANDA RETURN HUB
Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and the Netherlands will open a joint facility in Rwanda for rejected asylum seekers.
ASIA-PACIFIC
JAPAN SCRAMBLES JETS OVER RUSSIAN PLANES
Two suspected Russian aircraft enter Japanese airspace. Tokyo protests strongly and sends fighters up.
UNITED STATES
PENTAGON FAILS ITS AUDIT — NINTH YEAR
Auditors again cannot verify accounts covering $4.8 trillion in assets. The department fails for the ninth year running.
UNITED NATIONS
128 NATIONS MEET ON KILLER ROBOTS
Delegates gather in Geneva to weigh a treaty banning lethal autonomous weapons.
