## The stories

- **Lead — Ukraine brings down the Crimea Bridge arch.** A second strike in two days drops the Kerch Bridge's road arch into the strait; the rail span stands damaged and traffic is halted. Kyiv calls it payback for Russian strikes on its infrastructure; Moscow's missiles hit Kyiv's river bridges.
- **Tankers struck near the Strait of Hormuz.** Oil tankers are hit and catch fire by the strait; an Iranian drone attack on a U.S. radar base in Bahrain is intercepted, with no damage.
- **Trump, Xi and Putin to meet in Shenzhen.** China confirms the first three-way meeting of the three leaders, on Thursday at the APEC summit.
- **Five nations plan a Rwanda return hub.** Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and the Netherlands will open a joint facility in Rwanda for rejected asylum seekers.
- **Japan scrambles jets over Russian planes.** Two suspected Russian aircraft enter Japanese airspace; Tokyo protests strongly.
- **Pentagon fails its audit — ninth year.** Auditors again cannot verify accounts covering $4.8 trillion in assets.
- **128 nations meet on killer robots.** Delegates gather in Geneva to weigh a treaty banning lethal autonomous weapons.

Every story was checked against the day's reporting (Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera and others). The image is original work: a front page laid out in HTML and drawn as flat vector art, rendered to `entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# writes out/entry.png — a 1024x1024 PNG, byte-identical on every run
```

`main.ts` copies the image beside it to the output directory; `design.html` is the source the image was rendered from.
